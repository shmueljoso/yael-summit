The idea

From a single star to a whole network

Running a school can be lonely. This year the summit doesn't end when the day does: it connects principals by the challenges they face and the strengths they bring — from registration onward.

  1. Before the summit

    Everyone lights a star

    At registration, each principal shares what they're working on, what they're good at and what they want to learn. We suggest three first connections — people worth meeting before you arrive.

  2. At the summit

    The stars meet

    Two participants scan each other's badges and a new line appears on the map. A big screen shows the network growing live, and discussion circles are formed around shared challenges.

  3. After the summit

    The network keeps shining

    Small peer groups by topic, a short monthly check-in, and a bank of solutions born in conversation — so the connections last beyond the day.

      At the summit

      The hall becomes a night sky

      Ideas for the day itself — each one built on the same site and the same network.

      The living star wall

      A big screen in the hall shows the network growing. Every new introduction draws a line, every discussion circle lights up a constellation. At the end of the day — one picture of every connection made.

      Tap to connect

      Every badge has a personal QR code. Scan each other to save the contact and add a line to the map — no business cards.

      Matched discussion circles

      Tables of 6–8 principals grouped in advance by a shared challenge, with an opening question and a Foundation facilitator.

      The question hour

      Principals post a real question from their school; peers who've solved it tap “I can help”.

      My summit plan

      The schedule on your phone, with a nudge: “who to meet at the next break”, based on your connections.

      After the summit

      Constellations that stay

      Peer groups

      4–6 principals around one topic, a 45-minute video call once a month. The Foundation opens it; the principals lead it.

      “How we solved it”

      Short solutions from the conversations, tagged by topic — easy to find when the same problem reaches another school.

      My star, next year

      Your profile is saved. At the next summit, see how your personal network has grown and who joined it.
